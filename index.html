    About

    I am an undergraduate researcher in the Nonlinear Dynamics Lab at UNIST, advised by Prof. Pilwon Kim. I build data-driven digital twins of nonlinear dynamical systems using reservoir computing.

    My current work addresses a practical question about critical transitions: given only the statistics we can actually measure, how far can a system drift before its normal dynamics break down? To answer it, I am developing statistically conditioned digital twins (SCDT).

    So far I have applied this framework to ecological collapse in a chaotic food chain, voltage collapse in a power system, explosive synchronization in oscillator networks, and seizure onset in the Epileptor model.

    Next, I plan to tackle multivariate time-series problems using knowledge distillation and parallel reservoirs.

    Interests: nonlinear dynamics · reservoir computing · tipping points · knowledge distillation
